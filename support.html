Support

Questions or problems? Write to get.innerlist@gmail.com. Please never send your phrase or your photos.

How do I open my gallery?

Type your phrase into the search bar on the list. When it matches, a "Vault gallery found" row appears; tap it and confirm with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. Anything that doesn't match simply searches your list.

I forgot my phrase. Can you recover it?

No. Your phrase is never stored anywhere, so no one, including us, can recover it or open the gallery without it. This is deliberate: a phrase that can be recovered can also be stolen.

How do I add photos and videos?

Inside a gallery, tap +. Or, in Photos or any other app, tap Share, choose Innerlist and enter the gallery's phrase. Items are copied in; you then choose whether the originals are removed from Photos (iOS keeps removed items in Recently Deleted for 30 days).

How do I back up or move to a new iPhone?

In a gallery, open the … menu and export a backup. It is a single encrypted file that you can keep in Files or on a drive; restore it on any iPhone with the same phrase. Nothing is backed up automatically, and deleting the app deletes every gallery.

Can I have more than one gallery?

Yes, with the full version. Each gallery has its own phrase, and galleries cannot see each other. You can keep one with harmless photos to show if you are pressed.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the plans and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you bought with. To manage or cancel a subscription: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
