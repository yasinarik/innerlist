Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

In short: Innerlist collects no data. It has no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no code that connects to the internet. What you put in it stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

  • Your galleries. Photos and videos you add are encrypted on your iPhone (AES-256-GCM) with a key made from your secret phrase. We never receive them and cannot open them.
  • Your phrase. It is never stored, not on your device and not anywhere else. That is why no one, including us, can recover it if you forget it.
  • Your to-dos, habits and notes. Stored on your iPhone only.

Permissions

  • Photos. Used to import the items you choose, to remove the originals from your library only when you ask, and to save an item back to Photos when you ask.
  • Face ID or Touch ID. Checked by iOS when you open a gallery. The app never sees your biometric data.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not see your payment details. The app only asks iOS whether your Apple ID owns a purchase.

Backups

Nothing is backed up automatically, and galleries are excluded from iCloud and device backups. If you export a backup, it is an encrypted file that goes only where you save or send it, and opens only with your phrase.

Deleting your data

Delete a gallery from its menu, or delete the app: every gallery and list on the device is removed with it, for good.

Children

Innerlist does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
